# Exam Countdown Timer

Built this because I kept forgetting how many days were left for each exam. Now I just open this and everything is right there — color coded, live ticking, no excuses.

---

## What it does

- Add your exams with a name and date/time
- Live countdown showing days, hours, minutes, and seconds
- Color coded urgency — green (plenty of time), orange (1 week left), red (3 days or less)
- Summary bar showing total exams, urgent ones, and completed ones
- Saves everything in your browser — data stays even after you close the tab.

---

## How to run it

Just open `exam_countdown.html` in your browser. That's it!

No setup. No install. Works offline.

---

## How to use it

1. Type your exam name
2. Pick the date and time
3. Click **Add exam**
4. Watch the panic set in

To remove an exam, click the × button on its card.

---

## Built with

Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Uses `localStorage` to save your exams between sessions.
